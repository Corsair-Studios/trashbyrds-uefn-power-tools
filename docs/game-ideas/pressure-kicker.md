# Pressure Kicker

**Status:** Idea
**Platforms:** UEFN, Roblox
**Origin:** Spin-off of the field goal event in [One Shot Rogue](one-shot-rogue.md)

## Pitch
A field goal kicking game that runs on the engine's physics. Read the wind, judge the distance, and put it through the uprights.

## Core mechanics
- **Physics-driven ball:** the engine handles the flight. No canned animations.
- **Kick input:** aim (direction), power, and maybe a timing/accuracy meter.
- **Wind:** direction and strength change between kicks and are shown with a flag or indicator.
- **Distance:** kicks move farther back as the player progresses.

## Possible modes
- Streak mode: keep kicking until you miss.
- Clutch mode: game-winning kicks with the clock at 0:00.
- Head-to-head: players alternate kicks and push the distance back each round.

## Open questions
- How reliable and consistent is physics simulation in UEFN versus Roblox for a projectile like this?
- Should the player control the kicker in third person, or use a fixed kicking camera?
- How much randomness from wind feels fair and how much feels unfair?
